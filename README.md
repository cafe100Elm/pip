# Pip the Notchbot

A tiny robot that lives in your MacBook's notch. Its eyes change shape with its mood. It peeks out when you hover, chats when you click, DJs your Spotify or Apple Music, keeps a timer and tells you the weather.

## Download

**[Download Pip](https://github.com/cafe100Elm/pip/releases/latest/download/Pip.zip)**. Needs macOS 14 or later. Best on a MacBook with a notch.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded **Pip.zip** and drag **Pip** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Double-click Pip. macOS says it can't check Pip for malicious software, because Pip isn't from the App Store. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to the note about Pip. Confirm with your password. You only do this once.
4. Pip appears in your notch, and a little eyes icon appears in your menu bar.

Pip updates itself. When a new version comes out, Pip tells you.
