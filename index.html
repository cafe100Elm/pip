      What Pip does

      Each card has a button that shows it on the laptop above.

      Shape-shifting eyes

      Pip's eyes morph into arches, hearts, stars and winks, with blushes and body language. Poke its eyes a few times and it goes from shy to grumpy to dizzy. Leave it alone for 30 seconds and it dozes off.

      Chat and memory

      Ask Pip for a joke, music or a timer. Say “remember that…” and Pip keeps the note on your Mac, even after a restart. Pip answers with friendly scripted replies; for real conversations, turn on Pip's AI in Settings.

      Weather

      Ask “what's the weather?” or “weather in Paris” and Pip shows a little forecast card. Its eyes react: sparkles for sun, raindrops for rain, and shivers when it's freezing.

      DJ Pip

      Works with Spotify or Apple Music. Pip puts on headphones, bops along and comments on each song. Heart the songs you love and turn on synced lyrics. Close the panel and a spinning record sits beside the notch. This page plays Pip's own demo tunes.

      Quiet ads

      When a Spotify ad comes on, Pip turns your Mac's sound off and looks grumpy until it's over, then turns the sound back on. It doesn't skip or block the ad. If your Mac was already muted, Pip leaves it that way.

      Timer buddy

      Pick 1, 5, 15 or 25 minutes. Pip's eyes charge up, then drain like an hourglass while the countdown sits beside the notch. It sweats the last 10 seconds and rings until you click it.

      Get Pip

      Pip is free, made for friends. It's a small download that lives in your menu bar and your notch.

      Download PipPip.zip · for Apple silicon Macs
      Needs
      A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Macs with Intel chips aren't supported.
      Best on
      A MacBook with a notch. On other Macs, Pip sits at the top center of the screen.
      Music
      Spotify or Apple Music (for DJ Pip)
      AI brain
      Optional. Sign in with your email (invite-only for now), or use your own Claude API key.

      Install it in four steps

      1. Unzip it.

        Open your Downloads folder and double-click Pip.zip. You get the Pip app. (Safari often unzips it for you.)

      2. Move it.

        Drag Pip into your Applications folder.

      3. Open it.

        Double-click Pip. The first time, macOS shows a box called “Pip” Not Opened, saying Apple could not verify that Pip is free of malware. Click Done. Don't click the blue Move to Trash button: that one deletes Pip.

      4. Allow it, once.

        Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Pip. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway again. Pip pops out of your notch.

      Why the extra step?

      Pip isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks for your OK the first time. You only do it once. After that, Pip keeps itself up to date: when a new version is ready, Pip peeks out and tells you, and one click installs it.

      Pip may ask you

      • To control Spotify or Music. Click OK so DJ Pip can play, pause and skip songs.
      • To use your location. Click Allow so Pip can tell you the weather where you are. Rather not? Type your town in Pip's Settings instead.

      Good to know

      Settings

      Click the eyes icon in your menu bar, then Settings…, to turn on Pip's AI, set your town, turn on lyrics, or see what Pip remembers.

      Your stuff stays on your Mac

      Pip keeps its notes, chat and hearts on your Mac. Weather comes from Open-Meteo and lyrics from LRCLIB. When Pip's AI is on, your chats go to Claude, through Pip's helper (which keeps only message counts, never the chats) or straight from your Mac with your own key.

      Quitting

      Click the eyes icon in the menu bar and choose Quit Pip.

      Removing Pip

      Quit Pip, then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
